Why businesses choose us

Questions we get asked

How much does a website cost?

Business websites start at $2,000 and online stores at $3,500. After a free call you get a fixed quote, agreed before any work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business sites launch in two to four weeks once we have your content. Online stores usually take three to five weeks.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You receive the complete source code at handover. You can host it anywhere and have anyone work on it — there is no platform holding it hostage.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every build ships with technical SEO: fast load times, clean markup, structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console setup. For competitive searches we also run ongoing SEO, Google Business Profile and Google Ads.

Do you build websites for medical and therapy practices?

Yes — much of our work is for physicians, psychologists, therapists and wellness practices, including secure intake and screening forms through HIPAA-compliant providers such as Jotform.

Do you only work with businesses in Queens?

No. We are based in Rego Park and work across all five boroughs and Long Island — in person or over video — and remotely with clients outside New York.

What happens after my site launches?

Every project includes 30 days of support. After that, care plans cover hosting, updates and changes, and you still reach Elan directly by phone or text.
