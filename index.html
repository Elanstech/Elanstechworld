Questions we get asked

What does Elan's Tech World do?

We are a full-service studio in Rego Park, Queens. We design and build websites, run SEO, Google Ads and Google Business Profiles, film and manage Instagram, edit video and photos, design logos and brands, print business cards and flyers, make indoor signs and QR stands, and build property management systems.

How much does a website cost?

Business websites start at $2,000 and online stores at $3,500. Other services are quoted after a free call, and every quote is a fixed price agreed before work starts.

Can you manage our whole online presence?

Yes. Many clients hand us everything — website, Google Business Profile, SEO, Instagram and print — so it all stays consistent and they have one person to call.

Do you come to us to film for Instagram?

Yes. We write the scripts and shot lists, film on-site across New York City and Long Island, edit the reels and post them.

Do you design and print business cards and signs?

Yes — business cards, flyers, banners, circle logo signs, glass signs and QR stands are designed here and produced for you. Outdoor signs are available as part of a bundle.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every build ships with technical SEO: fast load times, clean markup, structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console setup. For competitive searches we also run ongoing SEO, Google Business Profile and Google Ads.

Do I own my website and content?

Yes. You receive the complete source code of your website, and every video, photo and design we make for you is yours.
