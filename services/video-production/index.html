Cinematic video & photo questions

Will a video slow down my website?

Not when it's done right. We compress and stream the video, show a still image first, and keep the file small enough that pages still load quickly on phones.

Can you edit footage and photos we already have?

Yes. Send what you have and we'll edit, color-match and cut it into versions for your site and social.

Where do you film?

Across New York City and Long Island, at your location.
