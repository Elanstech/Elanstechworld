Signs: circle, glass & QR stands

The moment someone walks in, your space should confirm everything your website promised. A clean circle sign on the door or a glass logo wall behind the front desk does that instantly.

QR stands do the quiet work: one scan to leave a Google review, follow your Instagram, see a menu or book the next visit. Outdoor storefront signs are available when they're part of a branding or website bundle.

Signs & QR stands questions

Do you make outdoor signs?

Yes, but only as part of a bundle with branding or a website. Indoor signs and QR stands can be ordered on their own.

What can a QR stand link to?

Anything: a Google review page, your Instagram, a menu or price list, a booking page or a special offer. With a dynamic code, the link can be changed later without reprinting.

Do you design the sign too?

Yes — design, proofs and production, matched to your logo and brand colors.
