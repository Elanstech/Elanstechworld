Why us

New York businesses hire us instead of a template builder, five separate vendors or a 40-person agency. Here's the honest version of why.

The short answer

Who is Elan's Tech World?
Elan's Tech World is a full-service creative and digital studio based in Rego Park, Queens, serving all five boroughs of New York City and Long Island. Elan Cohen leads every project personally.
What do you actually do?
Websites, SEO and Google Ads, Google Business Profiles, Instagram filming and management, cinematic video and photo editing, logos and branding, PR, business cards and flyers, circle and glass signs, QR stands and property management systems.
What makes you different?
One team runs your whole presence, and every website is hand-coded from a blank page — no WordPress themes, no Wix, no page builders. You receive the complete source code at handover.
What does it cost?
Business websites start at $2,000 and online stores at $3,500. Everything else is quoted after a free call as a fixed price, with no long-term contract.

Six reasons, and how to check each one

  1. No templates. Ever.

    Every site starts as an empty file: no theme bloat, no plugin security holes, and no competitor two neighborhoods over running the same layout.

    Check it: open any project in our portfolio and browse it live.

  2. You own it outright.

    At handover you get the complete source code. Host it anywhere, hire anyone to change it, keep it forever.

    Check it: source-code handover is written into every agreement, not sold as an upgrade.

  3. Built to be found.

    Structured data, clean markup, fast loads, a sitemap and Search Console are part of the build, not a phase bolted on at the end.

    Check it: run any of our client sites through Google PageSpeed Insights.

  4. You get Elan, not a queue.

    The person who scopes your project writes the code and answers the phone. No account managers, no ticket numbers, no junior handoff.

    Check it: (929) 417-6819 is a cell phone, not a switchboard.

  5. One team for all of it.

    Website, SEO, Instagram, video, logo, business cards and signs — one brand standard from the search results to the front door, and one number to call when anything needs changing.

    Check it: every logo in our portfolio was designed here, then carried through the site.

  6. Local, and it matters.

    We're in Rego Park. We know what New Yorkers search for, how local rankings work, and we can meet you in person.

    Check it: most of our clients are a subway or LIRR ride away.
