◆The Short Answer

If you only read one screen.

Who are we?

Elan's Tech World is a web design and digital marketing studio based in Rego Park, Queens, serving all five boroughs of New York City and clients nationwide. Elan Cohen designs and builds the work himself.

What makes us different?

Every website is hand-coded from a blank page — no WordPress themes, no Wix, no Squarespace, no page builders — and every client receives the complete source code at handover.

What does it cost?

Business websites start at $2,000 and e-commerce stores at $3,500. There is no monthly platform fee to keep your site online, and no contract is required to work with us.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support and Elan's personal cell number. Ongoing SEO and Google Ads management is available month to month, and your ad account always stays in your name.
