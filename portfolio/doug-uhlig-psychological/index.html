Doug Uhlig Psychological Services

Dr. Doug Uhlig, PhD runs two practices from one site: evidence-based therapy across three NYC offices, and the Manhattan Executive Coaching Consortium (MECC).

Industry
Therapy & executive coaching
Location
Manhattan, Brooklyn & Queens
What we did
Website design, Development, Intake forms, SEO
Browse the live site Visit dougpsychassociates.com
dougpsychassociates.com

The brief

Two audiences — people looking for a therapist and leaders looking for a coach — had to land on one site and each find their path in a single click.

What we built

  • A split home page that sends visitors to therapy or executive coaching
  • A four-part leadership intake quiz for coaching clients
  • A directory of the full clinical team, plus coach profiles
  • Location cards for the Manhattan, Brooklyn and Rego Park offices with directions
  • Services, team, coaching and contact pages
  • Contact forms and a link to his Psychology Today profile
