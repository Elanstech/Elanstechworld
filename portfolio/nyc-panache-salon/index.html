NYC Panache Salon

A luxury hair salon in Forest Hills for color, balayage, keratin smoothing, extensions, nails and waxing, led by founder and master stylist Marina Iskiyaeva.

Industry
Luxury hair salon
Location
Forest Hills, Queens
What we did
Website design, Logo design, Development, Online booking, SEO
Browse the live site Visit nycpanachesalon.com
nycpanachesalon.com

The brief

Panache's work is visual, so the site had to show it — real transformations, a clear price list and booking one tap away — with the gold-and-cream polish of the salon itself.

What we built

  • A priced service menu for cuts, color, smoothing, extensions, nails and waxing
  • Drag-to-compare before-and-after sliders and a filterable gallery
  • Online booking through Vagaro
  • A cinematic video hero
  • An FAQ page, Instagram feed and contact form
  • An llms.txt file so AI search tools describe the salon accurately
