The brief

Dr. Aronov wanted a site that feels as calm and clinical as her treatment room, puts the physician review front and center, and screens patients for safety before anyone books.

What we built

  • An editorial home page with a clinic-tour video and a scroll-driven infusion menu
  • A page for every infusion: what's in it, how long it takes, the price, and its own FAQ
  • A free online eligibility screening — contact details first, then yes/no safety questions — sent to HIPAA-compliant Jotform
  • A 40-answer FAQ and a research page with published references
  • The doctor's story, press coverage and a guided tour of the clinic
  • Ongoing Instagram, Google Business Profile and marketing management
