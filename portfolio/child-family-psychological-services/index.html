Child & Family Psychological Services

Therapy, evaluations and family court services for children, teens, adults and families since 2007, led by Dr. Kristie Doheny, PsyD.

Industry
Psychology practice
Location
Long Island & Queens
What we did

Browse the live site Visit childandfamilypsychservices.com
childandfamilypsychservices.com

The brief

Parents often arrive worried and unsure what kind of help they need. The site had to feel warm, explain a long list of services in plain language, point people to the right office, and keep forensic referrals on their own line.

What we built

  • Eleven service guides, from child therapy to custody evaluations and neurofeedback
  • Clinician profiles with full bios
  • A tabbed FAQ across five topics, plus an insurance section
  • Four office cards — Hauppauge, Forest Hills, Patchogue and Mineola — with one-tap directions
  • A separate contact line for forensic and court-related referrals
  • A training institute section with the play-therapy workshop season and registration
  • Office and event structured data for local search
