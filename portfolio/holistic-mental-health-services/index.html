Holistic Mental Health Services

Individual, couples and family therapy across four offices and telehealth statewide, founded by Dr. Kristie Doheny.

Industry

The brief

A sister practice to Child & Family with its own identity — calmer, focused on adults, and built around an $80 self-pay offer for daytime sessions.

What we built

  • An $80 self-pay offer with a live clock that shows whether today's booking window is open
  • Service guides for individual, couples and family therapy, CBT, EMDR, mindfulness, groups and neurofeedback
  • Therapy P.L.U.S., a dedicated LGBTQ+ affirming program
  • A founder profile and team directory
  • A filterable FAQ, crisis resources and four office cards with directions
