Park City 3 & 4

A cooperative of six 14-story buildings in Rego Park, established in 1955 and managed by AKAM.

Industry
Residential co-op
Location
Rego Park, Queens
What we did
Website design, Development, Online forms
Browse the live site Launching soon
parkcity3-4.vercel.app

The brief

Replacing the co-op's old WordPress site with one place for everything residents need — forms, building rules, payments and amenities — that also shows off the grounds.

What we built

  • Nine online resident forms — alterations, garage waiting list, pet registration and more — delivered straight to management
  • Resident guides for ClickPay payments, moves and deliveries, insurance, house rules and sublets
  • Amenity pages for the pool, playground, garages, storage and laundry
  • A 48-photo gallery organized into buildings, interiors, amenities and grounds
  • A directory of all six buildings and a resident FAQ
