Century One Management

A property management company on Queens Boulevard in Rego Park managing residential, commercial and luxury properties across Queens and NYC, led by founder and CEO Zarina Yusupova.

Industry
Property management
Location

The brief

Owners need to trust a manager before they hand over a building. The site had to explain the service plainly, show the portfolio and turn ad traffic into consultation requests.

What we built

  • Service pages for residential, commercial and luxury management
  • A properties page with filters, sorting and detail views
  • Tenant and owner portal links through DoorLoop
  • A dedicated ad landing page with a lead form
  • A how-it-works process, areas served and FAQ
