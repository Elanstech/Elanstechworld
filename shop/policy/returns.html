Return Policy

We stand behind every device. If something is not right, we make it right.

Last updated: March 2026
The short version: You have 14 days from delivery to return your device for a refund. The device must be in the same condition you received it. Contact us, ship it back, and we process your refund within 5 business days.

14-Day Return Window

All purchases from ETW Shop are eligible for return within 14 calendar days of the delivery date. To be eligible, the device must be returned in the same condition it was received, including all original accessories and packaging we shipped with it.

To Start a Return

Contact us by text, call, or email. We will confirm eligibility and provide return shipping instructions. We try to respond within a few hours during business days.

  • Text or call: (929) 417-6819
  • Email: elan@elanstechworld.com

Condition Requirements

To qualify for a full refund, devices must be returned in the condition they were delivered. We understand normal handling during the return window, but we do inspect every return upon receipt.

EligibleNot Eligible
Device in same condition as delivered Physical damage that was not present at delivery (cracks, dents, water damage)
All included accessories returned Missing accessories, cables, or packaging

Refund Process

Once we receive your return and confirm it meets the conditions above, we will process your refund to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5-7 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

Shipping costs are non-refundable. If a device is returned in a condition materially different from how it was shipped, we reserve the right to apply a restocking assessment of up to 15% or decline the return. We will always contact you first if there is an issue.

Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different device, simply return the original and place a new order. Inventory on refurbished devices is limited and moves quickly, so we recommend ordering your replacement promptly.

Defective Devices

If your device arrives with a defect or a functional issue that was not disclosed in the listing, contact us immediately. Defective devices are covered under our Limited Warranty and may be eligible for repair, replacement, or refund at our discretion, even beyond the 14-day return window.

Final Notes

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Any changes apply to purchases made after the revision date. We do our best to make every transaction smooth. If anything feels off about your order, reach out to us directly and we will work with you to find a fair solution.
